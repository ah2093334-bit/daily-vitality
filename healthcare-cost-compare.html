How It Works

This tool provides estimated out-of-pocket costs for common health scenarios in the US and UK. For the US, costs are highly variable based on insurance, location, and facility, so we provide a general estimate for uninsured or high-deductible situations. For the UK, the NHS provides comprehensive care, meaning most services are free at the point of use for residents, though private options exist. Our estimates are for illustrative purposes to highlight the systemic differences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are US costs so much higher?

The US healthcare system is primarily market-driven, with a mix of private insurance, government programs, and out-of-pocket payments. Prices for services are not standardized and can vary wildly between providers, even within the same city. Factors like insurance deductibles, co-pays, and lack of coverage for certain services contribute to high out-of-pocket expenses. The UK, on the other hand, operates largely under the National Health Service (NHS), which is publicly funded through taxation, making most services free at the point of use for eligible residents.

Are these actual costs I would pay?

No, these are estimates for illustrative purposes only. Actual costs can vary significantly based on many factors: your specific insurance plan (in the US), the exact medical facility, complications, post-care needs, and individual circumstances. For the UK, while NHS services are generally free, there might be charges for prescriptions, dental care, or eye care, and private healthcare options have their own costs. Always consult with healthcare providers and insurance companies for precise cost information.

What about insurance in the US?

Our US estimates generally reflect out-of-pocket costs for individuals with high deductibles or no insurance, aiming to show a "worst-case" scenario for comparison. If you have comprehensive health insurance in the US, your out-of-pocket expenses would typically be lower, limited by your deductible, co-pays, and out-of-pocket maximums. However, even with insurance, costs can still be substantial depending on your plan and the services received.
