How It Works

The Mindful Eating Check-in helps you become more aware of your body's signals. By rating your hunger before eating and your fullness after, you can start to identify patterns in your eating habits. This practice encourages you to eat when truly hungry and stop when comfortably satisfied, fostering a healthier relationship with food and your body. It's a simple yet powerful step towards mindful eating.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Hunger and Fullness Scale?

The Hunger and Fullness Scale is a tool used to help you tune into your body's natural hunger and fullness cues. It's a spectrum from extreme hunger (1) to extreme fullness (10), designed to help you recognize when you're truly hungry, when you're satisfied, and when you've eaten past the point of comfort.

How do I use this tool effectively?

Before you eat, pause and rate your hunger. After you finish eating, pause again and rate your fullness. The goal isn't perfection, but awareness. Over time, this practice can help you identify patterns, understand your body's signals better, and make more mindful eating choices.

Is this a diet tool?

No, this is not a diet tool. It's an educational and awareness tool for mindful eating. It encourages you to listen to your body rather than following external rules or restrictions. It's about building a healthier relationship with food and your body, not about weight loss or calorie counting.
