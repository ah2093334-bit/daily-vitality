Frequently Asked Questions

What is a habit stack?

A habit stack is a strategy where you link a new habit to an existing one. The idea is to use a habit you already do automatically as a trigger for the new habit you want to build.

How do I choose the best place to stack my habit?

Consider the timing and context of your existing habits. A good stack often involves placing the new habit immediately before or after an established routine that makes sense logically or temporally.

What if I have many existing habits?

If you have multiple existing habits, think about which one is most consistent and which new habit would flow most naturally from it. You can also experiment with different stack points to see what works best for you.
