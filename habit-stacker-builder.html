How It Works

Habit stacking is a powerful technique where you link a new habit to an existing one. By performing your new habit immediately after your trigger habit, you create a clear cue and make it easier to remember and implement. This tool helps you identify a strong trigger and list your desired new habits, then presents a clear, actionable plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a trigger habit?
A trigger habit is an action you already do regularly and reliably, like brushing your teeth, making your morning coffee, or getting into bed. It serves as the cue for your new habit.
How many habits can I stack?
You can stack as many habits as feel manageable. It's often best to start with one or two new habits and gradually add more as they become ingrained. The key is consistency.
What if I miss a day?
Don't worry about perfection! If you miss a day, simply get back on track with your next scheduled opportunity. The goal is progress, not flawless execution. Forgive yourself and restart.
