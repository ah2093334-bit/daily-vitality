Frequently Asked Questions

How is my Gut Health Score calculated?

Your score is estimated based on your daily intake of fiber-rich foods and fermented foods. We consider recommended daily targets for these categories to provide a personalized assessment. This is an educational estimate, not a medical diagnosis.

What are fiber-rich foods?

Fiber-rich foods include fruits, vegetables, whole grains (oats, brown rice, quinoa), legumes (beans, lentils), nuts, and seeds. These foods are crucial for digestive health and feeding beneficial gut bacteria.

What are fermented foods?

Fermented foods are foods that have been through a process of lacto-fermentation, where natural bacteria feed on the sugar and starch in the food, creating lactic acid. Examples include yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi, kombucha, and tempeh. They can introduce beneficial microbes to your gut.

Is this tool medical advice?

No, this tool provides an educational estimate only and is not intended as medical advice. Always consult with a healthcare professional for personalized health guidance and before making any changes to your diet or lifestyle.
