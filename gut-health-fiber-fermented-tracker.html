How It Works

This tool estimates your daily gut health score based on your intake of fiber-rich foods and fermented foods. Fiber feeds beneficial gut bacteria, while fermented foods introduce diverse microbes. Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "serving" for fiber-rich foods?

A serving is generally equivalent to:

  • 1 medium fruit (apple, banana) or 1 cup berries
  • 1 cup raw leafy greens or 1/2 cup cooked vegetables
  • 1 slice whole-grain bread or 1/2 cup cooked whole grains (oats, brown rice)
  • 1/2 cup cooked legumes (beans, lentils)
  • 1/4 cup nuts or seeds

Aim for a variety of these foods throughout the day.

What are examples of fermented foods?

Common fermented foods include:

  • Yogurt and Kefir (dairy or non-dairy)
  • Sauerkraut and Kimchi
  • Kombucha
  • Tempeh and Miso
  • Some aged cheeses (though probiotic content varies)

Look for products labeled "live and active cultures" or "unpasteurized" for the most probiotic benefits.

How often should I use this tracker?

You can use this tracker daily to monitor your intake and identify patterns. Consistent tracking for a few days can give you a good average. It's a great way to stay mindful of your dietary habits and make gradual improvements.
