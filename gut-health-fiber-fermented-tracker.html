How It Works

This tool estimates your "Gut Health Score" by evaluating your daily intake of two key categories: fiber-rich foods and fermented foods. Fiber is crucial for feeding beneficial gut bacteria, while fermented foods introduce diverse probiotics. The score is a simple aggregation, designed to highlight areas where you might increase your intake for better gut support. It's a guide, not a diagnostic, to encourage mindful eating for digestive wellness.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a "serving" of fiber-rich food?

A serving can vary, but generally, it's about 1/2 cup of cooked vegetables, beans, or whole grains, 1 medium piece of fruit, or 1 cup of leafy greens. The goal is to accumulate diverse sources throughout the day.

What are examples of fermented foods?

Common examples include plain yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi, kombucha, miso, and tempeh. Look for products that specify "live and active cultures" and avoid those with excessive added sugars.

How accurate is this score?

This score is a simplified estimate based on general nutritional recommendations. It does not account for individual health conditions, specific dietary needs, or the full complexity of gut microbiome health. It's a motivational tool to encourage healthy eating habits, not a precise medical assessment.
