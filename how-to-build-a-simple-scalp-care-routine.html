How to Build a Simple Scalp-Care Routine

Quick overview

How to Build a Simple Scalp-Care Routine: start gentle, use the right basic product, protect after cleaning, watch how your body responds.

Use the routine as a starting point, then adapt it to your own schedule. For health-related symptoms or medical concerns, professional advice takes priority over general online guidance.
