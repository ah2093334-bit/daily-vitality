Frequently Asked Questions

Can supplements replace a healthy diet?

No. While supplements can help fill nutritional gaps, they are not a substitute for a balanced, whole-food diet. Nutrients from food are often absorbed more effectively and come with additional beneficial compounds not found in isolated supplements. Always prioritize a diverse and healthy diet.

How long does it take to see results from diet changes for hair?

Hair growth is a slow process. It typically takes at least 3-6 months of consistent dietary changes and/or supplementation to notice visible improvements in hair health, as new hair needs time to grow out. Patience and consistency are key.

Is this tool suitable for everyone?

This tool provides general educational information. Individual nutritional needs can vary greatly due to genetics, lifestyle, health conditions, and medications. It is always best to consult with a healthcare professional or registered dietitian for personalized advice, especially if you have specific health concerns or dietary restrictions.
