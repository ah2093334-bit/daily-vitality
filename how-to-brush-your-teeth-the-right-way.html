How to Brush Your Teeth the Right Way

How-to guide•4 practical steps•3 min read

Quick overview

This step-by-step guide walks you through 4 steps: brush on a schedule, clean between teeth, freshen the full mouth and protect the result.

Use this article as a structured starting point. It is educational and not a diagnosis, but it is designed to help you act with more confidence and less confusion.

The steps

  1. Brush on a schedule
  2. Clean between teeth
  3. Freshen the full mouth
  4. Protect the result

Step 1: Brush on a schedule
