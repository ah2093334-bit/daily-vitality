How to Manage Stress Step by Step

How-to guide•4 practical steps•3 min read

Quick overview

This step-by-step guide walks you through 4 steps: notice the trigger or moment, use a short grounding action, reset your next decision and build a repeatable routine.

Use this article as a structured starting point. It is educational and not a diagnosis, but it is designed to help you act with more confidence and less confusion.

The steps

  1. Notice the trigger or moment
  2. Use a short grounding action
  3. Reset your next decision
  4. Build a repeatable routine
