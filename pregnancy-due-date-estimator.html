How it Works

This tool uses Naegele's rule, a common method for estimating a due date. It adds 40 weeks (280 days) to the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). This provides an estimated due date, but remember that babies arrive on their own schedule!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Naegele's Rule?

Naegele's rule is a standard method for calculating a pregnant person's estimated due date. It involves taking the first day of your last menstrual period, subtracting three months, and then adding seven days and one year.

Why is it an estimate and not exact?

Pregnancy length can vary. While 40 weeks is the average, full-term pregnancies can range from 37 to 42 weeks. This tool provides a helpful guideline, but individual variations are normal.

What if my cycle is irregular?

If your menstrual cycles are irregular, or if you are unsure about your LMP date, this calculation may be less accurate. Your healthcare provider can offer a more precise estimate, often using early ultrasound scans.
