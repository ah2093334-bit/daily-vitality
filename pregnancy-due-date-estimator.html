Pregnancy Due Date Estimator

Your simple guide to estimating your baby's arrival.

Enter Your Last Menstrual Period (LMP)

--

This is an estimated due date based on the Naegele's rule, which adds 40 weeks (280 days) to the first day of your last menstrual period.

0%

How it Works

This tool uses Naegele's rule, a common method for estimating a due date. It assumes a standard 40-week (280-day) pregnancy. Simply input the first day of your last menstrual period, and the tool calculates the estimated due date by adding 280 days to that date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an exact due date?

No, this is an estimate. Only about 5% of babies are born on their exact due date. Full-term pregnancy is considered between 37 and 42 weeks.

What if my cycle is irregular?

Naegele's rule works best for women with regular 28-day cycles. If your cycles are significantly longer or shorter, this estimate may be less accurate. Your healthcare provider can offer a more personalized estimate.

When should I see a doctor about my pregnancy?

It's recommended to contact your healthcare provider as soon as you suspect you are pregnant. They will confirm your pregnancy and discuss your prenatal care plan, including a more precise due date calculation.
