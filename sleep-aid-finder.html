How it Works

Simply tell us your sleep concern in the input box. Our tool analyzes your input and suggests common, evidence-aware non-medical sleep aids and techniques. It provides an estimated effectiveness for calmness, focus, and relaxation based on general knowledge. Remember, this is for informational purposes only.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this medical advice?

No, this tool provides general information and suggestions for common sleep issues. It is educational only and not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

What kind of sleep aids are suggested?

We focus on non-medical, lifestyle-based suggestions. This can include things like establishing a consistent sleep schedule, creating a relaxing bedtime routine, managing environmental factors (light, noise, temperature), mindfulness techniques, and dietary considerations. We do not suggest medications or supplements.

How are the effectiveness scores calculated?

The progress bars are illustrative estimates based on general understanding of how certain techniques are commonly perceived to impact calmness, focus, and relaxation for common sleep issues. They are not based on specific scientific studies for your individual input and should be seen as a guide, not a definitive measure.
