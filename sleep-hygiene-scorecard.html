How it Works

Our Sleep Hygiene Scorecard helps you understand your current sleep habits. By answering questions about your bedtime routine, sleep environment, and daily practices, you'll receive a personalized score. This score is an estimate to guide you toward better sleep hygiene.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is sleep hygiene?

Sleep hygiene refers to habits and practices that are conducive to sleeping well on a regular basis. Good sleep hygiene can help you fall asleep faster and improve the quality of your sleep.

How is the score calculated?

The score is calculated based on a weighted system that assigns points to answers reflecting good sleep hygiene practices. More positive habits contribute to a higher score.

Can this tool diagnose sleep disorders?

No, this tool is for educational purposes only and cannot diagnose sleep disorders. If you have persistent sleep problems, please consult a healthcare professional.
