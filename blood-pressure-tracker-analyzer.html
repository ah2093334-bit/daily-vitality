How It Works

Our Blood Pressure Tracker & Trend Analyzer helps you monitor your cardiovascular health by recording your systolic and diastolic blood pressure readings over time. Simply input your numbers and the date, and the tool will store them, display a visual trend graph, and calculate key metrics like average readings and trend directions. This allows you to easily spot patterns and fluctuations, providing valuable information to discuss with your doctor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I track my blood pressure?

The frequency depends on your individual health needs and your doctor's recommendations. For general tracking, once or twice a day at consistent times (e.g., morning and evening) is common. Always consult your healthcare provider for personalized advice.

What do the systolic and diastolic numbers mean?

Systolic (the top number) measures the pressure in your arteries when your heart beats. Diastolic (the bottom number) measures the pressure in your arteries between beats, when your heart rests. Both are important indicators of heart health.

Can this tool diagnose high blood pressure?

No, this tool is for educational purposes only and cannot diagnose any medical condition, including high blood pressure (hypertension). It helps you track your readings and visualize trends, which you can then discuss with a healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment. Always seek medical advice from a qualified doctor.
