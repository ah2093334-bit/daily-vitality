How It Works

Our Meal Prep Planner uses a curated database of healthy, balanced dinner recipes. Simply tell us how many meals you need, any dietary restrictions you have, and your preferred cooking time. We'll then generate a unique meal plan and a consolidated shopping list to make your week easier and healthier. It's designed to be flexible and inspire your healthy eating journey!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize the generated meals?

Currently, the tool generates a new set of ideas each time. If you don't like a suggestion, you can simply re-generate the plan. We're always looking to improve, so more customization options might be added in the future!

How are the "healthy" meals defined?

Our healthy meals focus on whole foods, lean proteins, plenty of vegetables, and balanced macronutrients. They are generally lower in processed ingredients, added sugars, and unhealthy fats, aligning with general wellness guidelines. Remember, this is for educational purposes only.

Is the shopping list comprehensive?

The shopping list includes the main ingredients required for the generated dinner ideas. It's a great starting point, but you may need to add staples you already have or personal preferences like spices and condiments. Always review it before heading to the store!
