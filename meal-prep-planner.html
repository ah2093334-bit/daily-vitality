How It Works

Our Meal Prep Planner uses your preferences to suggest quick, healthy dinner ideas from a curated database. We focus on balanced nutrition, simple ingredients, and efficient prep. It's designed to inspire your meal planning, making healthy eating easier and more enjoyable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the meal ideas generated?

Our tool selects meal ideas from a database of quick and healthy recipes. It filters these based on your preferred cooking time and any dietary restrictions you've selected, aiming for a balance of protein, vegetables, and healthy carbs.

Can I save or customize the meal ideas?

Currently, the tool provides a fresh set of ideas each time you generate. You can use the "Copy Results" button to save them to your clipboard or "Print Ideas" to get a physical copy for your meal planning.

Are the nutritional values provided?

While all suggested meals are designed to be healthy and balanced, specific nutritional values (calories, macros) are not provided by this tool. We focus on whole, unprocessed ingredients. For detailed nutritional information, please consult a registered dietitian or a dedicated nutrition tracking app.
