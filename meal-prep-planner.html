    How It Works

    Our Meal Prep Planner makes healthy eating simple. Just tell us how many dinners you need, any dietary preferences you have (like vegetarian or low-carb), and your typical cooking time. We'll then generate a personalized weekly meal plan with easy-to-follow dinner ideas and a convenient shopping list. It's designed to help you save time and eat well!

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What kind of "healthy" dinners does it suggest?

    Our generator focuses on balanced meals with whole foods, lean proteins, plenty of vegetables, and healthy fats. We prioritize "heart healthy dinner ideas" by including options rich in fiber, healthy fats, and nutrients, while being mindful of saturated fat and sodium. Examples might include baked salmon with roasted vegetables, lentil soup, or chicken stir-fry.

    Can I customize the meal plan further?

    Currently, the tool generates a plan based on the selected preferences. While you can't manually edit the generated plan within the tool, you can easily copy the results and adjust them in your preferred document or note-taking app. We recommend using the dietary preferences and cooking time filters to get the closest match to your needs.

    Is the shopping list comprehensive?

    The shopping list includes all the main ingredients needed for the suggested dinner ideas. It assumes you have common pantry staples like salt, pepper, cooking oil, and basic spices. Always review the list and compare it with your current pantry before heading to the store!
