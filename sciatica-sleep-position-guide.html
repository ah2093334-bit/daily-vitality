Frequently Asked Questions

Is sleeping on my back best for sciatica?

Sleeping on your back is often recommended because it allows for neutral spinal alignment. However, the best position is the one that causes you the least pain and discomfort. We provide tips for all positions.

What kind of pillows should I use?

Pillows are key for support. For back sleepers, a small pillow under the knees can help maintain the natural curve of the spine. Side sleepers often benefit from a pillow between the knees to keep hips aligned and reduce pelvic rotation.

Can my sleep position worsen sciatica?

Yes, certain positions can put direct pressure on the sciatic nerve or cause your spine to twist unnaturally, potentially exacerbating sciatica pain. Stomach sleeping, for example, often leads to neck and back strain.
