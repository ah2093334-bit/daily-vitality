    Frequently Asked Questions

    What kind of exercises will I receive?
    The exercises are generally simple, bodyweight movements focusing on stretching and strengthening key postural muscles, like shoulder blade squeezes, chin tucks, and gentle spinal twists.
    How often should I do these exercises?
    For best results, aim to incorporate the suggested exercises into your daily routine. Even a few minutes several times a day can make a significant difference. Listen to your body and adjust as needed.
    Can this tool diagnose posture problems?
    No, this tool provides general wellness information and suggestions based on your input. It is not a diagnostic tool and cannot replace professional medical advice. If you have concerns about your posture or experience pain, please consult a healthcare professional.
