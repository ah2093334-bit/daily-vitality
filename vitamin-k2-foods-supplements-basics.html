Vitamin K2: What It Does, Food Sources, and Supplement Basics

Quick overview

A practical guide to vitamin K2, food sources, supplement labels, and why medicines can change supplement decisions.

What this guide covers

  • The key idea in plain language
  • Practical habits
  • What common advice can and cannot tell you
  • When professional advice matters

What vitamin K2 is

Vitamin K2 refers to several menaquinone forms of vitamin K.

Food-first choices are a sensible starting point.

Food sources

K2 appears in some fermented foods, cheeses and animal foods.

Diet patterns matter more than chasing one single nutrient.

Supplements and medicines

Supplement labels may list MK-4 or MK-7.

People using medicines affected by vitamin K should discuss supplements with a clinician.

A practical approach

Use supplements only when there is a clear reason.

More is not automatically better.
