How It Works

The Grocery Budget Optimizer helps you allocate your weekly grocery budget across key healthy food categories based on your inputs. We use evidence-aware percentages for common healthy eating patterns, adjusting for the number of people and dietary preferences. This tool aims to provide a practical estimate to guide your shopping, helping you prioritize nutritious choices while staying within your budget. Remember, these are estimates for educational purposes, not medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the cost estimates?

The estimates are based on general healthy eating guidelines and average food costs, adjusted for your budget, number of people, and dietary preference. Actual costs can vary significantly based on your location, specific store prices, seasonal availability, and personal brand choices. This tool provides a helpful starting point for planning.

What dietary preferences are supported?

We currently support Standard (Mixed), Vegetarian, Vegan, and Pescatarian preferences. Each preference adjusts the allocation percentages for protein sources and other categories to reflect typical healthy eating patterns for that diet. We aim to add more options in the future.

Can I save or share my results?

Yes! You can use the "Copy Results" button to quickly copy the breakdown to your clipboard, making it easy to paste into a note or message. The "Print" button will open your browser's print dialog, allowing you to print or save the results as a PDF for future reference.
