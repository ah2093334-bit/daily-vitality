Frequently Asked Questions

What is nutritional yeast?

Nutritional yeast, often called "nooch," is a deactivated yeast that's popular in vegan cooking for its cheesy, nutty, and savory flavor. It's a good source of B vitamins, including B12 if fortified.

How do I use nutritional yeast?

Nutritional yeast can be sprinkled directly onto dishes like popcorn, salads, or pasta for a cheesy flavor boost. It's also a fantastic ingredient for making vegan cheese sauces, gravies, and as a flavor enhancer in soups and stews.

Is nutritional yeast healthy?

Nutritional yeast is a nutrient-dense food, particularly rich in protein and B vitamins. Many brands are fortified with B12, which is essential for nerve function and can be difficult to obtain from a vegan diet. It's a healthy addition to a balanced diet.
