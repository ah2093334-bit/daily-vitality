How It Works

Our Activity Calorie Burn Estimator uses standard metabolic equivalent (MET) values for various activities, combined with your age and weight, to provide an estimated calorie burn. METs represent the energy cost of an activity relative to resting metabolism. While useful for general guidance, individual results can vary based on factors like fitness level, intensity, and body composition. This tool is for educational purposes only.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are these calorie estimates?

These estimates are based on average metabolic equivalent (MET) values and are designed to provide a general idea. Actual calorie burn can vary significantly due to individual factors like metabolism, fitness level, environmental conditions, and the exact intensity of the activity. It's a useful guide, but not a precise measurement.

What are METs (Metabolic Equivalents)?

A Metabolic Equivalent of Task (MET) is a physiological measure expressing the energy cost of physical activities. One MET is defined as the energy expenditure of sitting quietly. An activity with a MET value of 3 means you're expending three times the energy you would sitting still. Our tool uses these values to calculate calorie burn.

Can I use this tool for weight loss planning?

While understanding calorie burn is part of weight management, this tool provides estimates for educational purposes only. For personalized weight loss planning, it's crucial to consult with a healthcare professional or a registered dietitian. They can provide tailored advice based on your individual health needs and goals.
