How It Works

Our Activity Calorie Burn Estimator uses standard Metabolic Equivalent of Task (MET) values for various activities. METs represent the energy cost of an activity relative to resting metabolism. We combine your body weight, the activity's MET value, and its duration to calculate an approximate calorie burn. The food equivalents are then provided to give you a relatable sense of the energy expended, based on average calorie counts for those items.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this calorie burn estimator?

This tool provides an estimate based on standard metabolic equivalent of task (MET) values and average body weight. Individual calorie burn can vary significantly due to factors like age, sex, fitness level, intensity, and environmental conditions. It's for educational purposes only and not medical advice.

What are METs (Metabolic Equivalents of Task)?

METs are a common way to express the energy cost of physical activities. One MET is defined as the energy expended while sitting quietly, which is roughly 1 calorie per kilogram of body weight per hour. Activities with higher MET values burn more calories.

Why are food equivalents shown?

Showing food equivalents helps to visualize the energy expenditure in a relatable way. It's a fun, educational comparison to understand the impact of your movement, but it's not intended as a dietary recommendation. Always consult a healthcare professional for personalized nutrition advice.
