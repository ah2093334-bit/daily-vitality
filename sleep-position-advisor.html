How it Works

Simply enter any sleep discomforts you're experiencing, like neck pain or acid reflux. Our tool analyzes your input and suggests the most beneficial sleep positions to alleviate your specific issues. Get personalized guidance for a more comfortable and restful night's sleep.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of discomforts can I enter?
You can enter a wide range of common discomforts, including physical pain (e.g., back pain, hip pain, shoulder pain), breathing issues (e.g., snoring, sleep apnea), digestive problems (e.g., heartburn, indigestion), and general discomforts like stiffness or feeling restless.
How accurate are the suggestions?
Our suggestions are based on general wellness principles and common recommendations for sleep positions related to specific discomforts. They are intended as helpful guidance. For persistent or severe issues, it's always best to consult with a healthcare professional.
Can I enter multiple discomforts at once?
Yes, you can enter multiple discomforts separated by commas or by listing them. The tool will try to find a position that offers the best compromise for all the issues you mention.
