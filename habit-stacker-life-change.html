    How It Works

    Simply list one or more existing daily habits you already do reliably. Then, add the new habits you wish to incorporate. Our tool will suggest ways to chain these together, making it easier to adopt new behaviors by linking them to established routines. It's a practical approach to building a more fulfilling and productive day.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    • What is a habit stack?

      A habit stack is a strategy where you link a new habit you want to form to an existing habit you already do consistently. For example, "After I brush my teeth (existing habit), I will drink a glass of water (new habit)." This makes it easier to remember and perform the new behavior.

    • Can I add multiple existing habits?

      Yes, you can add multiple existing habits. The tool will consider each as a potential anchor point for your new habits. You can then choose which existing habit you'd like to stack your new ones onto for the most effective routine.

    • Is this tool based on scientific evidence?

      The concept of habit stacking is a widely recognized behavioral psychology technique. While this tool provides an educational estimate for routine building, it is not a substitute for professional medical or psychological advice.
