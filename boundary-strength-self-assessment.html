Tips for Strengthening Your Boundaries:

Identify Your Limits: Understand what you are and aren't comfortable with in various situations (time, energy, emotional space).

Communicate Clearly: Use "I" statements to express your needs and limits calmly and directly, without apology or aggression.

Practice Saying 'No': Start with small, low-stakes situations. Remember, 'no' is a complete sentence.

Reinforce Your Boundaries: Consistency is key. Gently remind others of your boundaries if they are tested.

How It Works

This self-assessment helps you reflect on your current boundary-setting habits. By answering a few simple questions about your daily interactions, you'll receive an estimated boundary strength score and personalized tips. It's designed to be a starting point for self-awareness, empowering you to cultivate healthier relationships and protect your well-being. Remember, this is an educational tool, not a diagnostic one.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 'boundary strength'?

Boundary strength refers to your ability to set and maintain healthy limits in your relationships and interactions. It involves knowing your needs, communicating them clearly, and protecting your time, energy, and emotional well-being.

How is my score calculated?

Each question in the assessment has a weighted score based on how strongly the answer reflects healthy boundary setting. These scores are summed up to give you an overall boundary strength estimate.

Is this tool medical advice?

No, this tool provides an educational estimate only and is not a substitute for professional medical or psychological advice. If you have concerns about your mental health or relationships, please consult a qualified healthcare provider.
