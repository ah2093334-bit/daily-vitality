Daily Water Intake Calculator

Your daily fluid need depends on body size, how active you are and how hot it is. This calculator gives a practical estimate you can adjust.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your weight and choose the unit.
  2. Add your daily exercise time and climate.
  3. Press Calculate for your daily water target.

Frequently asked questions

Does coffee or tea count?

Yes, most drinks count toward fluid intake, and foods such as fruit and soup add water too.

Can I drink too much water?

Yes. Drinking far more than you need can be harmful. Spread intake through the day and follow medical advice if you have kidney or heart conditions.

How do I know I am hydrated?

Pale yellow urine and rarely feeling thirsty are good signs.
