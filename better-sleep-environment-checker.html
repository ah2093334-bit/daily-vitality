How It Works

Simply adjust the sliders to reflect your bedroom's current light, sound, and temperature conditions. The tool will then provide an estimated sleep environment score and offer personalized, practical tips to help you create a more conducive atmosphere for restful sleep.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal temperature for sleep?
Most experts recommend a bedroom temperature between 60-67°F (15.5-19.5°C) for optimal sleep. This cooler environment helps your body's core temperature drop, signaling it's time to sleep.
How much light is too much for sleeping?
Even small amounts of light can disrupt your circadian rhythm. Aim for a dark room, ideally using blackout curtains and avoiding electronic device screens.
Can noise really affect my sleep quality?
Yes, noise can cause micro-arousals that you might not even remember, leading to less restorative sleep. Consistent, low-level white noise can sometimes help mask disruptive sounds.
