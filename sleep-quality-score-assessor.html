How It Works

Our Sleep Quality Score tool helps you understand how various aspects of your evening routine might be impacting your sleep. By answering simple questions about factors like screen time, caffeine intake, and relaxation habits, the tool calculates an estimated score out of 100. This score reflects how conducive your current routine is to achieving restful sleep, providing insights into areas you might consider adjusting for better sleep health. It's a quick, practical way to assess and reflect on your pre-sleep behaviors.

How is the Sleep Quality Score calculated?

The score is an estimate based on common sleep science principles. Each factor in your evening routine (like screen time, caffeine, exercise, etc.) is assigned a weighted impact. These impacts are combined to give you a score out of 100, reflecting how conducive your routine is to good sleep. Higher scores indicate a more sleep-friendly routine.

Is this tool medical advice?

No, this tool provides an educational estimate only and is not medical advice. It's designed to help you reflect on your habits. Always consult with a healthcare professional for personalized medical advice or concerns about your sleep.

How can I improve my Sleep Quality Score?

Generally, reducing screen time before bed, avoiding caffeine and heavy meals late in the evening, ensuring regular but not intense exercise close to bedtime, and incorporating relaxation techniques can improve your score. Review the explanations provided with your result for specific areas to focus on.
