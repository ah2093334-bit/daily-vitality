How It Works

This tool uses your Last Menstrual Period (LMP) date to estimate your gestational age, conception date, and due date. We use the common assumption that ovulation occurs around day 14 of a 28-day cycle, and pregnancy is dated from the first day of your LMP. Your selected symptoms are then cross-referenced with common early pregnancy signs to provide a comprehensive overview. Remember, these are estimates and individual experiences can vary.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the estimated conception date?

The estimated conception date is based on the average menstrual cycle and ovulation timing. It's an approximation and can vary. For a precise date, consult a healthcare professional who can use medical imaging and other methods.

Should I rely on this tool for medical diagnosis?

No, this tool is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider for any health concerns or before making any decisions related to your health or pregnancy.

What if my symptoms don't match the common signs?

Every pregnancy is unique, and not everyone experiences the same symptoms or at the same time. The absence of certain symptoms doesn't necessarily mean you're not pregnant, nor does their presence confirm pregnancy. If you have concerns, please speak with your doctor.
