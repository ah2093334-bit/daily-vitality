How It Works

The Fast Sleep Planner creates a personalized wind-down routine based on your desired bedtime and preferred activities. It structures a 30-60 minute period before sleep, suggesting activities that promote relaxation and signal to your body that it's time to wind down. The goal is to gradually reduce stimulation and prepare your mind and body for a smoother transition into sleep.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a wind-down routine important?

A consistent wind-down routine helps signal to your body and mind that it's time to prepare for sleep. It reduces stress, calms your nervous system, and can improve sleep quality and the speed at which you fall asleep. It's like a gentle off-ramp for your busy day.

What kind of activities are best for winding down?

Activities that are calming and low-stimulation are ideal. Examples include reading a physical book, light stretching or yoga, journaling, listening to quiet music, taking a warm bath, or practicing mindfulness/meditation. Avoid screens (phones, tablets, TV), intense exercise, heavy meals, and caffeine/alcohol close to bedtime.

How long should my wind-down routine be?

Most experts recommend a wind-down routine of 30 to 60 minutes. This gives your body sufficient time to transition from wakefulness to sleep. Experiment with different lengths to find what works best for you, but consistency is more important than the exact duration.
