How It Works

The Daily Vitality Habit Tracker helps you build and maintain healthy routines. Simply list up to five habits you want to cultivate, then mark them as complete each day of the week. The tool visualizes your weekly progress with clear charts and calculates your current streak, motivating you to stay consistent. All your data is stored locally in your browser for privacy and ease of use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many habits can I track?

You can track up to 5 habits simultaneously with this tool. We recommend focusing on a small number of habits to maximize your chances of success and build strong foundations.

How is the streak calculated?

The streak is calculated for each individual habit based on consecutive days of completion. If you miss a day for a specific habit, its streak resets. The "Longest Current Streak" displayed is the highest current streak among all your active habits.

Is my data saved?

No, this tool does not save your data on our servers. All habit inputs and daily progress are stored only in your browser's local storage. This means your data is private to you, but if you clear your browser data, use a different browser, or switch devices, your progress will be lost.
