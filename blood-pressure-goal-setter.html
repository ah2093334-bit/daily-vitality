Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy blood pressure?

Generally, a healthy blood pressure is considered to be less than 120/80 mmHg. However, this can vary based on individual factors like age and existing health conditions. Our tool provides an estimated range tailored to your details.

How often should I check my blood pressure?

If you have high blood pressure or are at risk, your doctor might recommend checking it daily or several times a week. For general wellness, checking it periodically (e.g., once a month) can be a good practice. Always follow your doctor's specific advice.

Can lifestyle changes really impact blood pressure?

Absolutely! Lifestyle changes are often the first line of defense against high blood pressure. Regular exercise, a healthy diet (like the DASH diet), maintaining a healthy weight, reducing sodium, limiting alcohol, and managing stress can significantly improve blood pressure readings and overall heart health.
