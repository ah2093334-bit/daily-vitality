How It Works

The Inflammation-Fighting Food Finder helps you understand the general inflammatory potential of various foods. We classify common food items into categories: Anti-Inflammatory, Pro-Inflammatory, or Neutral, based on widely accepted nutritional science. This tool is designed to offer quick, practical insights for a healthier diet, always remember it's for educational purposes only.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tool classify foods?

The tool classifies foods based on general scientific consensus regarding their typical impact on inflammatory pathways in the body. This is a simplified guide and individual responses to foods can vary. It's for educational purposes only.

Is this tool suitable for medical diagnosis or diet planning?

No, this tool is for educational purposes only and is not intended to provide medical advice, diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian for personalized dietary advice and medical concerns.

What if a food isn't listed or I get an 'unknown' result?

Our database covers common food items. If a food isn't recognized, it means we don't have specific data for it in our simplified classification. For less common or complex foods, research from reliable sources or consult a professional is recommended. We aim to expand our database over time.
