How It Works

Frequently Asked Questions

What is sleep apnea?

Sleep apnea is a sleep disorder where breathing repeatedly stops and starts during sleep. These pauses can last for a few seconds to minutes and may occur many times an hour.

Is this a diagnostic tool?

No, this tool is for informational and educational purposes only. It highlights common symptoms associated with sleep apnea but cannot diagnose the condition. A proper diagnosis requires consultation with a healthcare professional.

What should I do if I suspect I have sleep apnea?

If you suspect you have sleep apnea, it's important to speak with your doctor or a sleep specialist. They can discuss your symptoms, conduct an evaluation, and recommend appropriate diagnostic tests if needed.
