What Resting Heart Rate Can — and Can’t — Tell You

Resting pulse is a useful signal, but symptoms and trends matter more than one isolated number.

Educational information only — not medical advice or diagnosis.

What a resting pulse reflects

For many adults, 60–100 beats per minute is a common resting range. Fitness, stress, temperature, hydration, medicines, and illness can shift it.

Why trends are useful

A pulse that is consistently different from your usual baseline can be more meaningful than one random measurement.

Why symptoms matter most

Chest pressure, fainting, severe breathing trouble, or sudden weakness should not be dismissed because a pulse number looks normal.
