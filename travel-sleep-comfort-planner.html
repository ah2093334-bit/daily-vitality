Frequently Asked Questions

What is jet lag?

Jet lag, or desynchronosis, is a temporary sleep disorder that occurs when your body's internal clock (circadian rhythm) is out of sync with the new time zone you've traveled to. This can cause fatigue, insomnia, digestive issues, and difficulty concentrating.

How can I minimize jet lag?

Key strategies include gradually adjusting your sleep schedule before travel, staying hydrated, avoiding alcohol and caffeine, getting natural light exposure at your destination, and following a consistent sleep-wake pattern upon arrival.

Is this tool a substitute for medical advice?

No, this tool is for informational and educational purposes only. It provides general recommendations based on common travel advice. Always consult with a healthcare professional for personalized medical advice, especially if you have underlying health conditions.
