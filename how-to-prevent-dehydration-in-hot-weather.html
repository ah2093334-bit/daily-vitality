How to Prevent Dehydration in Hot Weather

How-to guide•4 practical steps•3 min read

Quick overview

This step-by-step guide walks you through 4 steps: spread water across the day, watch the heat and activity level, use cues that make sense and replace what you lose.

Use this article as a structured starting point. It is educational and not a diagnosis, but it is designed to help you act with more confidence and less confusion.
