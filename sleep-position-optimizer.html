Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really possible to change my sleep position?

Yes, with conscious effort and consistency, you can gradually adjust your sleep position. It often involves using pillows strategically to support the new position until it becomes more natural. Be patient with yourself, as it can take time to break old habits.

What kind of pillow is best for side sleepers?

Side sleepers generally benefit from a firmer, thicker pillow that fills the gap between their ear and shoulder, keeping the head and neck aligned with the spine. Memory foam or latex pillows are often good choices for providing this support.

Can sleep position affect snoring?

Absolutely. Sleeping on your back can cause your tongue and soft palate to collapse to the back of your throat, obstructing your airway and leading to snoring. Side sleeping is often recommended to help keep the airway open and reduce snoring.

When should I consult a doctor about my sleep?

If you experience chronic pain, severe snoring, persistent fatigue, or suspect you have a sleep disorder like sleep apnea, it's crucial to consult a doctor or a sleep specialist. This tool provides general guidance, but professional medical advice is essential for diagnosis and treatment.
