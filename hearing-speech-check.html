Hearing & Speech Check

Listen to a short spoken word and choose what you heard. The words become quieter across the rounds, but the test stays within the volume set on your own device.

Safety: Set your device to a comfortable normal listening level before starting. Do not turn the volume up during the test. This is not an audiogram and cannot measure hearing thresholds in decibels.
Ready•6 rounds

Use a quiet room

Use your normal speakers or headphones at a comfortable conversational volume. Press Start, then use “Play word” for each round.

Listen carefully

If you cannot hear anything, choose “Couldn’t hear it.”
