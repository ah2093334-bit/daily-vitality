Frequently Asked Questions

Is this medical advice?

No, this tool provides general information and techniques for sleep improvement. It is educational only and not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

How are the techniques chosen?

The techniques are based on common sleep hygiene principles and widely recognized methods for addressing specific sleep challenges. We aim to provide practical, actionable advice.

Can I use this for children?

This tool is designed for adults. Sleep challenges and their solutions can differ significantly for children. Please consult a pediatrician or sleep specialist for advice regarding children's sleep.
