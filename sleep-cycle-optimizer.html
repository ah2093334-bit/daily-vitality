Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sleep cycle?
A sleep cycle is a period of sleep that lasts about 90 minutes. It includes different stages, from light sleep to deep sleep and REM (Rapid Eye Movement) sleep. Waking up at the end of a cycle can make you feel more refreshed.
Why is a bedtime window better than a specific time?
Sleep onset can vary slightly each night. A window provides flexibility, acknowledging that you might fall asleep a little earlier or later than planned, while still ensuring you get sufficient sleep before your wake-up time.
Can I adjust my sleep duration?
Yes, the recommended sleep duration is typically between 7-9 hours for adults. However, individual needs vary. You can adjust the input to see how different durations might affect your optimal bedtime.
