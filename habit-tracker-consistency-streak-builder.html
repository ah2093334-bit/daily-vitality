How It Works

The Daily Vitality Habit Tracker helps you build consistency by visualizing your progress. Simply input your new habit and its start date. Each day you complete your habit, mark it off to extend your streak. The tool calculates your current streak, total days tracked, and completion rate, providing motivational feedback to keep you going. Your data is saved locally in your browser, so it's always ready when you return.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start tracking a new habit?

Simply enter the name of your new habit in the text field, select the date you started (or plan to start), and click 'Start Tracking'. Your habit will be saved automatically, and you can then mark it as complete each day.

What happens if I miss a day?

Missing a day will break your current streak. Don't worry, this is a normal part of habit building! The tracker will help you visualize your consistency over time, and you can always start a new streak the next day. The goal is progress, not perfection.

Is my habit data saved?

Yes, your habit data is saved locally in your browser's storage (localStorage). This means it will be available each time you visit this tool on the same device and browser. Your data is not sent to our servers, ensuring your privacy.
