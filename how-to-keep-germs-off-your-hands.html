How to Keep Germs Off Your Hands

How-to guide•4 practical steps•3 min read

Quick overview

This step-by-step guide walks you through 4 steps: wet and soap thoroughly, scrub all surfaces, rinse and dry well and protect the clean result.

Use this article as a structured starting point. It is educational and not a diagnosis, but it is designed to help you act with more confidence and less confusion.

The steps

  1. Wet and soap thoroughly
  2. Scrub all surfaces
  3. Rinse and dry well
  4. Protect the clean result

Step 1: Wet and soap thoroughly

Start with clean running water, wet both hands, and cover palms, backs of hands, fingers and thumbs with soap. This step is most useful when you keep it simple, repeatable and matched to your real routine rather than trying to do everything at once.
