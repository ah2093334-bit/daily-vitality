How It Works

The Daily Vitality Mood Tracker is a simple, private tool designed to help you become more aware of your emotional patterns. Each day, you select your current mood and can add a brief note. Your entries are stored securely on your device, allowing the tool to generate a visual summary of your mood distribution over time. This can help you identify trends, understand triggers, and reflect on your emotional well-being.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Mood Tracker work?

Simply select the mood that best describes how you're feeling today from the options provided. You can also add a short note to capture more details about your day or specific thoughts. The tool then stores your entries locally in your browser and provides a visual summary of your mood trends over time, helping you observe patterns.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes, absolutely. All your mood entries and notes are stored exclusively on your device within your web browser's local storage. This means your data never leaves your computer or phone and is not sent to any servers. We do not track your usage or collect any personal information.

What if I want to delete my mood entries?

You can easily clear all your stored mood entries at any time. In the results section, there will be an option to 'Clear All Data'. Clicking this will permanently remove all your past check-ins from your browser's local storage. Please note this action cannot be undone.
