How It Works

The Kids' Plate Planner uses established dietary guidelines to suggest age-appropriate portion sizes for different food groups. By selecting your child's age and activity level, the tool provides a visual and quantitative estimate of what a balanced meal might look like. It aims to simplify healthy eating for busy parents, offering a practical starting point for meal planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are "food groups" and why are they important?

Food groups categorize foods with similar nutritional properties. They are important because consuming a variety of foods from all groups ensures your child gets a wide range of essential vitamins, minerals, protein, and energy needed for healthy growth and development.

How accurate are these portion suggestions?

These suggestions are general estimates based on common dietary guidelines for children within the specified age and activity ranges. They are designed to be a helpful starting point, but individual children's appetites and needs can vary. Always observe your child's hunger and fullness cues.

Can I use this for children with allergies or special dietary needs?

This tool provides general guidance and does not account for specific allergies, intolerances, or medical conditions. If your child has special dietary needs, please consult with a healthcare professional or a registered dietitian who can provide tailored advice.
