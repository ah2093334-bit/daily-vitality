How It Works

The Restorative Sleep Factor is a simplified metric designed to give you a quick overview of your sleep quality. It combines your reported sleep duration, how refreshed you feel upon waking, and the presence of common sleep disruptors. A higher score indicates potentially more restorative sleep. This tool is intended to raise awareness and offer actionable tips, not to replace professional medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the Restorative Sleep Factor calculated?

The factor is an estimate based on your reported sleep duration, how refreshed you feel upon waking, and the presence of common sleep disruptors. Longer, refreshing sleep with fewer disruptors leads to a higher factor. It's a simplified model for educational purposes.

Is this tool suitable for diagnosing sleep disorders?

No, this tool is for educational purposes only and provides a general estimate of sleep quality. It is not a diagnostic tool for sleep disorders. If you have concerns about your sleep, please consult a healthcare professional.

What can I do if my Restorative Sleep Factor is low?

A lower factor suggests areas for improvement. Focus on consistent sleep schedules, creating a comfortable sleep environment, avoiding caffeine/alcohol before bed, and managing stress. The personalized tips provided by the tool offer specific actions you can take.
