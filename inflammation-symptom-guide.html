    Frequently Asked Questions

    • What is inflammation?

      Inflammation is your body's natural response to injury or infection. It's a protective process, but chronic or prolonged inflammation can contribute to various health issues.

    • Can this tool diagnose my condition?

      No, this tool is strictly for educational purposes. It cannot diagnose any medical condition. Always consult with a qualified healthcare professional for any health concerns or before making any decisions related to your health or treatment.

    • What kind of lifestyle tips can I expect?

      Tips may include general recommendations on diet, exercise, stress management, and sleep. These are broad suggestions aimed at supporting overall wellness and may help manage inflammatory responses.
