Frequently Asked Questions

What is a micro-break?

A micro-break is a very short period of rest or pause taken during a longer period of activity, typically lasting from a few seconds to a few minutes. They are designed to help refresh your mind and body, reduce fatigue, and improve focus.

Can I adjust the break times after generation?

This tool provides an estimate based on your input. While it suggests optimal times, you are always welcome to adjust them to fit your personal flow and needs. The goal is to create a flexible plan that works for you.

Is this tool suitable for shift workers or irregular schedules?

This tool is best suited for individuals with a relatively consistent daily schedule. For highly irregular or shift-based work, it's recommended to use the generated plan as a starting point and adapt it manually to your specific working hours.
