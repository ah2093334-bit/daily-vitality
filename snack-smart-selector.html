Frequently Asked Questions

Are these snack suggestions suitable for everyone?

The snack suggestions are general recommendations for healthy adults. Individual dietary needs, allergies, and health conditions can vary. Always consult with a healthcare professional or registered dietitian for personalized dietary advice, especially if you have specific health concerns or dietary restrictions.

How often should I snack?

The frequency of snacking depends on your individual energy needs, activity level, and overall meal pattern. For some, 1-2 snacks per day can help maintain energy levels and prevent overeating at main meals. Listen to your body's hunger cues and choose nutrient-dense options when you do snack.
