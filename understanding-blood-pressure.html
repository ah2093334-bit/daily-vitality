Understanding Blood Pressure Numbers

A clear guide to systolic and diastolic pressure, common categories, and how to get a more useful home reading.

Educational information only — not medical advice or diagnosis.

What the two numbers mean

Systolic pressure is the top number and reflects pressure when the heart contracts. Diastolic pressure is the bottom number and reflects pressure while the heart relaxes between beats.

Why one reading is not the whole story

Stress, pain, caffeine, talking, movement, and poor technique can change a reading. Trends from properly taken readings are usually more useful than a single isolated result.

When a reading is urgent

Very high readings paired with chest pain, shortness of breath, weakness, numbness, vision changes, or difficulty speaking can require emergency care.
