Frequently Asked Questions

What makes these recipes "heart-healthy"?

Our recipes prioritize ingredients known to support cardiovascular health, such as lean proteins (fish, chicken, plant-based), whole grains, healthy fats (like olive oil), and a wide variety of fruits and vegetables. We aim to limit saturated and trans fats, excessive sodium, and added sugars.

Can I adjust the recipes for dietary restrictions?

While the tool provides general heart-healthy suggestions, individual dietary restrictions (e.g., allergies, gluten-free, dairy-free) may require modifications. Always review ingredient lists carefully and make substitutions as needed. If you have specific medical dietary needs, please consult a registered dietitian.

Are the cooking times accurate?

The cooking times provided are estimates for active preparation and cooking. Actual times may vary based on your cooking speed, equipment, and specific ingredient preparation. We focus on recipes generally achievable within the selected timeframe.
