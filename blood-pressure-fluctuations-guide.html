Frequently Asked Questions

Is this medical advice?
No, this tool is for educational purposes only and does not provide medical advice. Always consult with a qualified healthcare professional for any health concerns or before making any decisions related to your health or treatment.
What are normal blood pressure ranges?
Generally, a normal blood pressure reading is considered to be less than 120/80 mmHg. However, optimal ranges can vary based on individual health factors. This tool provides general context, not definitive diagnoses.
Can I trust the insights provided?
The insights are based on common associations and general health information. They are intended to be educational and may not reflect every individual's situation. Blood pressure can be influenced by many complex factors.
