Frequently Asked Questions

What are normal blood pressure ranges?

Generally, a normal blood pressure reading is considered to be less than 120/80 mmHg. However, what's considered normal can vary slightly based on individual factors. Always consult with a healthcare professional for personalized advice.

Is this tool a substitute for medical advice?

No, this tool is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

What do systolic and diastolic numbers mean?

Systolic pressure (the top number) measures the pressure in your arteries when your heart beats. Diastolic pressure (the bottom number) measures the pressure in your arteries when your heart rests between beats.
