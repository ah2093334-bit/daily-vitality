Frequently Asked Questions

What is nutritional yeast?
Nutritional yeast is a deactivated yeast, often used as a vegan cheese substitute. It has a distinct nutty, cheesy, umami flavor and is a good source of B vitamins.
Is nutritional yeast healthy?
Nutritional yeast is a good source of protein and several B vitamins, including B12 if fortified. It can be a nutritious addition to a balanced diet.
Can I use nutritional yeast in sweet dishes?
While most commonly used in savory dishes, a small amount of nutritional yeast can add a subtle depth or nutty undertone to some baked goods or desserts, though its primary appeal is savory.
