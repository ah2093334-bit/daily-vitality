Frequently Asked Questions

Are eggs really good for me?

Yes, eggs are a nutrient-dense food, packed with high-quality protein, essential vitamins (like B12, D, and A), and minerals (like selenium and choline). They can be a valuable part of a balanced diet for most people. The cholesterol in eggs has less impact on blood cholesterol levels than previously thought for healthy individuals.

Does preparation method affect nutrition?

While the core nutritional profile remains similar, preparation methods can cause minor changes. For example, frying eggs in oil adds fat and calories, while boiling or poaching keeps them lean. Some heat-sensitive vitamins might see a very slight reduction with prolonged high-heat cooking, but overall, the nutritional benefits remain largely intact.

What is "Daily Value" (DV)?

Daily Value (DV) is a reference amount of nutrients to consume or not to exceed each day. It's used on food labels to help consumers understand the nutrient content of foods within the context of a total daily diet. Our tool provides an estimated percentage of DV based on a 2,000-calorie diet, which may vary depending on individual needs.
