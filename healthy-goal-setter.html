How It Works

Simply input your current habits and what you hope to achieve. Then, select your primary goal type and the timeframe you're working with. Our tool will analyze your inputs to suggest a realistic and actionable goal designed to guide you towards your desired outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of goals can I set?
You can set goals related to weight loss, better sleep, increased energy, stress reduction, healthy eating, fitness, or any other health and wellness objective you have in mind.
Is this medical advice?
No, this tool is for educational purposes only. The goals generated are estimates and suggestions. Always consult with a qualified healthcare professional for any health concerns or before making any decisions related to your health or treatment.
Can I adjust the units (e.g., lbs vs. kg)?
Currently, the tool uses US customary units (e.g., pounds, hours). Unit toggles for specific metrics are not yet available but are a planned feature for future updates.
