How It Works

Simply enter your medication's name, the time you usually take it, and any relevant daily activities or food relationships. Our assistant analyzes this information against general wellness principles to suggest an optimal timing window. It considers factors like meal times and potential interactions to help you find the best slot for your medication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this medical advice?
No, this tool provides general suggestions based on common health principles and is for educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with your doctor or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.
What if I take multiple medications?
You can use this tool for each medication individually. It's a good idea to note down the suggestions for each and discuss them with your healthcare provider to ensure there are no potential interactions and that the timing works best for your overall regimen.
How are the "Optimal Window" and "Absorption Factor" calculated?
The "Optimal Window" is a general estimate of when a medication might be best absorbed or least likely to interfere with daily activities, based on common guidelines. The "Absorption Factor" is a simplified representation of how likely the medication is to be absorbed effectively, considering factors like food relation. These are educational estimates, not precise scientific measurements.
