Frequently Asked Questions

What is mindful eating?
Mindful eating involves paying full attention to the experience of eating and to your body's signals of hunger and fullness. It's about savoring your food and being aware of your eating habits without judgment.
Why is a slower eating pace beneficial?
Eating slower allows your brain time to register fullness signals, potentially preventing overeating. It also aids digestion by giving your stomach more time to process food and can increase enjoyment of your meal.
Is this tool medically accurate?
This tool provides an educational estimate for a mindful eating pace. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.
