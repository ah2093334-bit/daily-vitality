      Frequently Asked Questions

      Why is proper storage important for nutrient retention?

      Proper storage significantly slows down the degradation of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants in fruits and vegetables. Factors like temperature, humidity, and exposure to light or air can accelerate nutrient loss. Storing produce correctly helps maintain its freshness and nutritional value for longer.

      How does preparation affect nutrient levels?

      Preparation methods like peeling, cutting, and cooking can impact nutrient levels. Some nutrients are water-soluble and can leach out during boiling, while others are heat-sensitive. This tool provides tips to minimize nutrient loss during preparation, such as steaming instead of boiling, or consuming produce with its skin when appropriate.

      Is this tool suitable for all types of produce?

      This tool focuses on common fruits and vegetables, providing general guidelines. While it covers a wide range, specific exotic or less common produce might have unique requirements not fully detailed here. Always consult reliable sources for specialized produce.
