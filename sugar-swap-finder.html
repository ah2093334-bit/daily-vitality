How It Works

The Sugar Swap Finder helps you make informed choices by suggesting healthier alternatives to common sugary foods and drinks. Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a "healthier" sugar swap?

A healthier sugar swap typically involves reducing added sugars, increasing fiber content, and opting for whole, unprocessed foods. For example, choosing whole fruit over fruit juice provides more fiber and nutrients, while plain yogurt instead of sweetened yogurt significantly cuts down on added sugars.

Can I use this tool for all types of sugary foods?

This tool is designed for common sugary foods and drinks. While it aims to cover a broad range, very specific or niche items might not have direct suggestions. However, the principles of choosing whole foods and reducing added sugars can be applied broadly.

Are the suggested alternatives suitable for everyone?

The suggestions are general healthy eating recommendations. Individual dietary needs, allergies, and health conditions vary. Always consult with a doctor or registered dietitian for personalized advice, especially if you have specific health concerns or dietary restrictions. This tool provides educational information only, not medical advice.
