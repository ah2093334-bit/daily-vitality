How To Habit Tracker Notion: Build Your Daily Routine

Published 2026-11-13 · 5 min read

#habit tracking

Key takeaways

1Start small with 1-2 achievable habits to avoid burnout.
2Notion allows custom habit trackers with formulas for progress.
3Excel/Google Sheets offer simple, effective tracking with basic functions.
4Review your tracker weekly and adjust habits as your needs evolve.

How To Habit Tracker Notion: Your Daily Vitality Guide

To create a habit tracker in Notion, start by making a new database. Name it 'Habit Tracker' and add properties for 'Habit Name' (text), 'Type' (select: daily, weekly, monthly), and a 'Checkbox' property for each day of the week (e.g., 'Mon', 'Tue', 'Wed', etc.). You can then use formulas to track progress and visualize your consistency. This method provides a flexible and powerful way to monitor your routines.

Habit tracking is a simple yet powerful tool for personal growth and wellness. It involves recording whether you've performed a specific action each day, week, or month. This consistent logging helps build awareness, reinforces positive behaviors, and highlights areas where you might be struggling. Whether you're aiming to drink more water, meditate daily, or read more often, a well-designed habit tracker can be your accountability partner.

What Most People Get Wrong About Habit Tracking

Many people set up a habit tracker with too many habits at once, or they choose habits that are too large and overwhelming. This often leads to burnout and abandonment. The key is to start small, focus on one or two foundational habits, and ensure they are specific and achievable. For instance, instead of 'exercise daily,' try 'walk for 15 minutes after dinner.' Gradually add more habits as your initial ones become ingrained.

How To Make a Habit Tracker in Notion: Step-by-Step

Notion offers incredible flexibility for creating custom tools. Here's a detailed guide to building a robust habit tracker:

  1. Create a New Database: In your Notion workspace, click "+ New page" and select "Table - Full page" or "Table - Inline." Name it "Daily Habits."
  2. Define Your Habits: In the first column (default "Name"), list each habit you want to track (e.g., "Drink 8 Glasses Water," "Meditate 10 Mins," "Read 20 Pages").
  3. Add Daily Checkboxes: For each day of the week, add a new property. Click the "+" button, select "Checkbox" as the type, and name it after the day (e.g., "Mon," "Tue," "Wed," "Thu," "Fri," "Sat," "Sun").
  4. Add a "Total Done" Formula (Optional but Recommended): This helps you see your weekly progress at a glance. Add a new property, select "Formula" as the type, and enter the following formula:
    if(prop("Mon"), 1, 0) + if(prop("Tue"), 1, 0) + if(prop("Wed"), 1, 0) + if(prop("Thu"), 1, 0) + if(prop("Fri"), 1, 0) + if(prop("Sat"), 1, 0) + if(prop("Sun"), 1, 0)
    This formula counts how many days you checked off for each habit.
  5. Add a "Progress Bar" Formula (Optional): For a visual representation, add another "Formula" property. If you have 7 daily checkboxes, your formula might look like this:
    slice("███████", 0, prop("Total Done")) + slice("░░░░░░░", 0, 7 - prop("Total Done")) + " " + format(round(prop("Total Done") / 7 * 100)) + "%"
    This creates a simple progress bar with a percentage.
  6. Create a "Weekly View" (Optional): To reset your tracker weekly, you can duplicate this database each week or create a master database of weeks that links to daily habit entries. For simplicity, many users just duplicate the page weekly and reset checkboxes.

How To Habit Tracker Dominic Hartt Style

Dominic Hartt, a popular Notion creator, often emphasizes simplicity and automation. His approach to habit tracking in Notion typically involves a master "Habits" database linked to a "Daily Log" database. Each day, you create a new entry in your "Daily Log," and through relations and rollups, you can check off habits for that specific day. This method is more advanced but allows for powerful historical tracking and aggregation of data over time. It's ideal for those who want to analyze trends and see long-term consistency.

How To Make Habit Tracker in Excel or Google Sheets

If Notion feels too complex, Excel or Google Sheets are excellent alternatives for creating a simple habit tracker. Here's how:

How To Make Habit Tracker in Excel

  1. Set up Columns: In cell A1, type "Habit." In B1, C1, D1, etc., type the dates for each day of the month or week (e.g., "Jan 1," "Jan 2," etc.).
  2. List Habits: In column A, starting from A2, list your habits (e.g., "Meditate," "Exercise," "Read").
  3. Track Progress: For each habit and date, simply type "X" or "1" if you completed it, or leave it blank if you didn't.
  4. Add a "Total" Column: In the last column, use the COUNTIF function to count the "X"s for each habit across the row (e.g., =COUNTIF(B2:AF2, "X") for a month).
  5. Conditional Formatting (Optional): Select your tracking cells (B2:AFX), go to "Conditional Formatting" > "Highlight Cells Rules" > "Text that Contains..." and set it to highlight cells containing "X" with a green fill.

How To Make Habit Tracker in Google Sheets

The process for Google Sheets is almost identical to Excel. The functions and steps are largely the same. You can also leverage Google Sheets' sharing features to collaborate on habit tracking with a friend or family member for added accountability.

How To Use a Habit Tracker Effectively

Setting up the tracker is only half the battle. Effective use is crucial:

  • Keep it Visible: Place your tracker where you'll see it daily. For Notion, pin it to your sidebar or make it a linked database on your dashboard. For physical trackers, put it on your fridge or desk.
  • Be Honest: Don't check off a habit if you didn't genuinely complete it. The goal is accurate self-assessment, not a perfect streak.
  • Review Regularly: At the end of each week, take a few minutes to review your progress. Celebrate successes and identify patterns in your struggles. What made it hard to complete certain habits?
  • Adjust as Needed: Your habits and goals will evolve. Don't be afraid to modify your tracker, add new habits, or remove ones that no longer serve you.
  • Focus on Consistency, Not Perfection: Missing a day isn't a failure; it's an opportunity to get back on track tomorrow. The "don't break the chain" mentality can be motivating, but don't let a broken chain derail your entire effort.

Who This Is NOT For

While habit tracking is beneficial for many, it's not a magic bullet. If you're struggling with significant mental health challenges, chronic procrastination, or deep-seated behavioral issues, a habit tracker alone might not be sufficient. In such cases, seeking guidance from a therapist, coach, or medical professional is a more appropriate first step. A tracker is a tool to support existing motivation and structure, not to create it from scratch in difficult circumstances.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest way to make a habit tracker?

The easiest way is often a simple pen-and-paper checklist or a basic spreadsheet in Excel or Google Sheets, where you list habits and mark them off daily.

Can I track multiple habits in Notion?

Yes, Notion is excellent for tracking multiple habits. You can list each habit as a row in a database and add columns for each day of the week to check them off.

How often should I review my habit tracker?

It's beneficial to review your habit tracker weekly to assess progress, identify challenges, and adjust your approach. A monthly review can also help you see longer-term trends.

Is a digital habit tracker better than a physical one?

Neither is inherently better; it depends on your preference. Digital trackers offer automation, data analysis, and portability, while physical trackers provide a tangible, screen-free experience.
