How It Works

Our "Egg-cellent Protein & Micronutrient Breakdown" tool simplifies understanding the nutritional value of eggs. Simply input the number of large eggs you consume, and we'll calculate the estimated protein, key vitamins like B12 and D, and essential minerals such as selenium. We use up-to-date average nutritional data for large eggs to provide you with a quick and easy overview of your intake. It's a handy way to see how eggs contribute to your daily nutrient goals!

Frequently Asked Questions

What size egg does this tool use for calculations?

This tool uses the nutritional data for a "large" chicken egg (approximately 50g or 1.76 oz) as its basis for all calculations. Nutritional values can vary slightly with different egg sizes (e.g., medium, extra-large, jumbo).

Are these values exact, or are they estimates?

The values provided are estimates based on average nutritional data. Actual nutrient content can vary slightly due to factors like the hen's diet, breed, and cooking method. This tool provides a helpful general guide rather than precise scientific measurements.

Why are only certain nutrients shown?

We focus on key nutrients for which eggs are a significant source and that are commonly of interest for daily wellness, such as protein, Vitamin D, Vitamin B12, and Selenium. Eggs contain many other nutrients in smaller amounts, but these are highlighted for their notable contribution.
