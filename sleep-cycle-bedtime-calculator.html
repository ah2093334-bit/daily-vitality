Sleep Cycle Bedtime Calculator

Sleep runs in cycles of roughly 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle usually feels easier than waking in the middle of deep sleep.

How to use this calculator

  1. Pick the time you must wake up.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Choose a bedtime from the list; 5 to 6 cycles suits most adults.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours of sleep do adults need?

Most adults need 7 to 9 hours a night.

Is the 90-minute cycle exact?

No. Cycle length varies from person to person and through the night, so treat the times as a guide.

What if I cannot fall asleep fast?

Add the extra minutes to your bedtime and keep a steady routine.
