Daily Protein Intake Calculator

Protein needs depend on body weight and goal. This calculator uses commonly cited ranges per kilogram of body weight.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your weight.
  2. Select the goal that matches you.
  3. Press Calculate for a daily range in grams.

Frequently asked questions

Can too much protein hurt?

Very high intakes may strain people with kidney disease. Check with a clinician if you have a medical condition.

Which foods are high in protein?

Eggs, fish, poultry, dairy, beans, lentils, tofu and nuts.

Do I need protein powder?

Not necessarily. Food can cover your needs; powder is just convenient.
