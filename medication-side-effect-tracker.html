How It Works

The Medication Side Effect Tracker is a simple, private tool designed to help you keep a record of your medication experiences. When you take a new medication or notice a reaction, simply enter the medication name, describe the side effects, and note the date. Your entries are saved directly in your browser, creating a personal log that you can review at any time. This organized record can be invaluable for discussions with your healthcare provider, helping them understand your responses to treatments more clearly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tracker help me?

This tracker helps you maintain a personal log of your medications and any side effects you experience. By consistently logging this information, you can identify patterns, remember details, and have a clear record to share with your doctor, making your appointments more productive.

Is my data private?

Yes, absolutely. All data you enter into this tool is stored only in your web browser's local storage. It is not sent to our servers or any third parties. This ensures your personal health information remains entirely private and under your control.

Can I use this tool instead of consulting a doctor?

No, this tool is for educational and personal tracking purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider for any health concerns or before making any decisions about your medication or health regimen.
