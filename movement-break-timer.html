How It Works

The Movement Break Timer helps you integrate regular activity into your workday. Simply set your desired interval for breaks and how long each break should last. When the timer goes off, a notification will appear with a simple, desk-friendly stretch or movement idea. This helps combat the negative effects of prolonged sitting and keeps you feeling more refreshed and focused.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I take a movement break?

Experts often recommend taking a short break (5-10 minutes) every 30-60 minutes of sitting. Our timer allows you to set intervals that work best for your schedule and needs.

What kind of movements will the timer suggest?

The timer provides simple, desk-friendly stretches and light activities like neck rolls, shoulder shrugs, wrist circles, standing up and stretching, or a quick walk to grab water. The goal is to break prolonged sitting.

Can I use this tool on my phone?

Yes, this web tool is designed to be fully responsive and works well on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers. You can keep it open in a tab and receive notifications.
