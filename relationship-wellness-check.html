How It Works

Answer a few simple questions about your relationship's communication and shared experiences. Your responses are used to provide an estimated wellness score and personalized suggestions for strengthening your bond. It's a quick way to reflect and identify areas for growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool for romantic relationships only?
While designed with romantic partnerships in mind, the principles of communication, shared activities, and mutual understanding are valuable in any close relationship, such as friendships or family bonds.
What do the scores mean?
The scores are an estimate based on your answers, ranging from 1 to 5 for each category (Communication and Connection). Higher scores indicate stronger wellness in that area. The overall score is a sum of these, providing a general indication of your relationship's current state.
What if I get a low score?
A lower score is an opportunity for growth, not a judgment. It highlights areas where you and your partner might focus your energy. Consider discussing the results openly and exploring the suggestions provided to find practical ways to improve.
