How it Works

Simply enter an existing habit you already do consistently. Our generator will then suggest a new, complementary habit that you can "stack" onto it. This means you perform the new habit immediately after your existing one, making it easier to remember and adopt.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is habit stacking?

    Habit stacking is a strategy where you link a new habit you want to form to an existing habit you already do. For example, if you want to start meditating, you could stack it by saying, "After I finish my morning coffee, I will meditate for 5 minutes."

  • How are the suggestions generated?

    The suggestions are based on common wellness practices and logical connections between activities. We aim to provide practical and achievable ideas that can genuinely support your health and well-being goals.

  • Can I use this for any habit?

    Yes, you can use this tool for any existing habit, whether it's a morning routine, a work-related task, or an evening wind-down activity. The more specific your input, the more tailored the suggestion will be.
