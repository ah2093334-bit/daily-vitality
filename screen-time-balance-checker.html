How it Works

Simply input the estimated number of hours you spend on different digital activities each day. The tool then calculates a score based on a balanced approach to screen time, considering productive versus leisure use.

This provides a general overview of your digital habits and encourages mindful engagement with technology.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered "balanced" screen time?

A balanced approach typically prioritizes productive screen time (work, learning) over purely recreational activities. There's no single magic number, as it depends on individual needs and goals.

Can I use this for my children?

This tool is designed for general adult self-assessment. For children, it's best to consult with a pediatrician or child development specialist for age-appropriate screen time guidelines.

What if my total screen time is very high?

If your total screen time is high, it might be beneficial to explore ways to reduce it, such as setting time limits, scheduling tech-free periods, or finding offline hobbies. Small, consistent changes can make a big difference.
