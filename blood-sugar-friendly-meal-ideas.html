Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a meal "blood sugar friendly"?

Generally, meals that are lower in refined carbohydrates and added sugars, and higher in fiber, protein, and healthy fats, tend to have a slower impact on blood sugar levels. This helps to avoid rapid spikes and crashes.

Are these meal ideas personalized?

These are general suggestions based on common dietary preferences and blood sugar management principles. For personalized advice, it's always best to consult with a healthcare professional or registered dietitian.

Can I adjust the portion sizes?

Yes, the macronutrient breakdowns are estimates for a typical serving. You can adjust portion sizes based on your individual needs and guidance from your healthcare provider.
