How It Works

Simply enter your baby's age in months and select their primary sleep challenge from the dropdown. Our tool will then provide an estimated overview of typical sleep patterns and age-appropriate considerations for that stage.

This information is designed to help you understand general developmental trends and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age range does this tool cover?

This tool covers babies from 0 to 36 months (0 to 3 years old). Sleep patterns and needs change significantly during this period, and the tool aims to provide relevant insights for each stage.

How accurate are these sleep stage estimations?

These are general estimations based on common developmental milestones and sleep research. Every baby is unique, and their sleep journey may vary. This tool provides a helpful guide, not a definitive diagnosis.

When should I consult a doctor about my baby's sleep?

If you have significant concerns about your baby's sleep, if their sleep issues are causing distress to you or your baby, or if you suspect an underlying medical condition, it's always best to consult with your pediatrician or a certified sleep consultant.
