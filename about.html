About Daily Vitality

Daily Vitality shares simple, honest wellness habits that actually fit into everyday life.

What we publish

Plain-language articles on nutrition, sleep, movement, gut health, skin, heart health, mental wellbeing and everyday routines; free browser-based tools such as a water planner, sleep calculator and habit tracker; and practical digital guides for people who want to go deeper.

How we create content

Every article is written to be clear, practical and cautious. We use AI-assisted drafting and editorial checks, and we avoid miracle claims, guarantees and fear-based language. Articles are educational summaries — they are not written or reviewed by a doctor unless a page says so explicitly.

What we do not do

  • We do not diagnose conditions or replace medical care.
  • We do not promise cures, quick fixes or guaranteed results.
  • We do not recommend stopping or changing prescribed medication.

Founder

Daily Vitality was started by Wafadar to share simple, honest wellness habits and to build useful free tools and guides for everyday readers.

Products & transparency

Some pages link to our own digital guides sold through Gumroad. When we mention a product, it is our own unless clearly stated otherwise.

Contact

Questions, corrections or ideas? Use the contact page. If you spot an error in an article, please tell us and we will review it.
