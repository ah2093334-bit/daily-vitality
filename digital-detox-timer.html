How It Works

Simply set your desired screen-free duration using the hours and minutes inputs. Click "Start Break" to begin your timer. You'll receive a gentle alert when your time is up, encouraging you to step away from your devices and engage in real-world activities. It's a simple yet effective way to build healthier digital habits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pause the timer?
This timer is designed for focused, uninterrupted breaks. While there isn't a pause button, you can always reset it if an urgent task arises and start again later.
What kind of offline activities do you suggest?
Anything that helps you disconnect and recharge! This could include reading a book, going for a walk, spending time with loved ones, practicing a hobby, meditating, or simply enjoying a quiet moment.
Is this tool tracking my usage?
No, this tool operates entirely within your browser and does not track your screen time or any personal data. Your privacy is paramount.
