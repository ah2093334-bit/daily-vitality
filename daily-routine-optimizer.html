How It Works

Simply input your current daily activities, your main goals, and your typical energy and sleep levels. Our tool uses this information to provide an estimated breakdown of your day, suggesting adjustments to help you achieve a better balance between focused work, rest, and personal growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool providing medical advice?

No, this tool is for educational and informational purposes only. It provides estimates and suggestions for routine optimization based on the data you provide. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

How are the suggestions calculated?

The suggestions are based on general principles of time management, productivity, and well-being. We consider factors like your stated goals, energy levels, sleep duration, and work hours to suggest a balanced allocation of time across different life areas. The percentages are estimates designed to guide you towards a more structured day.

Can I adjust the suggested times?

This tool provides an initial estimate. You are encouraged to use these suggestions as a starting point and adjust them to fit your personal preferences, lifestyle, and specific daily demands. The goal is to inspire a more intentional approach to your routine.
