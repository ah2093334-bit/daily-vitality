Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of snacks are suggested?

Suggestions focus on whole foods rich in nutrients that can support mood and energy, such as fruits, nuts, seeds, yogurt, and dark chocolate. We avoid processed foods and excessive sugar.

Can these snacks really boost my mood?

Certain nutrients found in healthy foods, like omega-3 fatty acids, B vitamins, and magnesium, are linked to brain health and can play a role in mood regulation. While not a cure, mindful snacking can be a supportive practice.

Is this a substitute for professional medical advice?

Absolutely not. This tool is for informational and educational purposes only. If you have concerns about your mood, energy levels, or diet, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
