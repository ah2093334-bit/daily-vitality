Privacy Policy

Daily Vitality uses browser storage for requested features such as saved reads, tool preferences, and your cookie-consent choice. These features can work without advertising cookies.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose to allow optional analytics in the cookie panel. If you reject optional analytics, the analytics script is not loaded by our consent system.

Location and weather

The local weather feature asks your browser for location permission. You can deny that permission and continue using the site. Daily Vitality does not need your precise location for ordinary browsing.

Health tools

Most browser-based tools process entries on the page itself. Do not enter information you consider sensitive unless a tool clearly explains how it is handled.

Your choices

Use the “Cookie settings” control at the bottom of the site to change optional analytics preferences.
