Frequently Asked Questions

What is nutritional yeast? 🤔

Nutritional yeast is a deactivated yeast, often a strain of Saccharomyces cerevisiae, sold as flakes or powder. It's known for its savory, cheesy, umami flavor and is a popular ingredient in vegan and vegetarian cooking. It's also a good source of B vitamins, and often fortified with B12.

How should I store nutritional yeast? 📦

To maintain its freshness and potency (especially for B vitamins), store nutritional yeast in an airtight container in a cool, dark place, such as a pantry or cupboard. Avoid exposure to light, heat, and moisture, which can degrade its nutritional content and flavor.

Can I use nutritional yeast if I have a yeast allergy? ⚠️

While nutritional yeast is deactivated and different from the active yeast used in baking, individuals with severe yeast allergies or sensitivities should exercise caution. It's always best to consult with a healthcare professional or allergist before introducing new foods if you have known allergies. (Educational only, not medical advice).
