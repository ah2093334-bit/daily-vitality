How It Works

This calculator uses a common guideline to estimate your daily water needs. It starts with a baseline based on your body weight and then adjusts for your activity level and the climate you're in. Higher activity and hotter, drier climates generally require more water.

Remember, this is a simplified model. Individual needs can vary significantly based on health, diet, and other factors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is hydration important?

Water is essential for nearly every bodily function, including regulating body temperature, transporting nutrients, lubricating joints, and removing waste. Proper hydration supports energy levels, cognitive function, and overall well-being.

Can I drink too much water?

Yes, it's possible to drink too much water, a condition called hyponatremia. This is rare and usually occurs with extreme overconsumption over a short period, often in endurance athletes. For most people, drinking to thirst is a safe approach.

Does this include water from food?

This calculator primarily estimates fluid intake from beverages. About 20% of our daily water intake typically comes from food. You can consider this goal as a target for your drinks, and your food will contribute to the rest.
