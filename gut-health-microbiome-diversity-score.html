Frequently Asked Questions

What is microbiome diversity?

Microbiome diversity refers to the variety of different types of microorganisms (bacteria, fungi, viruses) living in your gut. A diverse microbiome is generally associated with better overall health, stronger immunity, and reduced risk of various diseases.

How can I improve my gut health?

Key strategies include eating a wide variety of plant-based foods rich in fiber, incorporating fermented foods, limiting processed foods and sugar, managing stress, getting enough sleep, and regular physical activity. Avoiding unnecessary antibiotic use is also important.

Is this score a medical diagnosis?

No, this tool provides an educational estimate based on common wellness principles and is not a medical diagnosis. For accurate assessment of your gut health or any medical concerns, please consult with a qualified healthcare professional.
