How It Works

Simply enter your desired wake-up time and list the key activities you'd like to include in your morning. Our tool will then estimate the time needed for each activity and create a structured checklist, helping you make the most of your mornings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I need more or less time for an activity?
The time estimates are suggestions. Feel free to adjust them in your generated routine or when planning your next day. The goal is a practical routine that works for you.
Can I add more activities later?
Absolutely! You can always come back, add more activities to the list, and regenerate your routine. Experiment to find the perfect balance for your mornings.
Is this a medical recommendation?
No, this tool provides an educational estimate for building a morning routine. It is not medical advice. Always consult with a healthcare professional for personalized health guidance.
