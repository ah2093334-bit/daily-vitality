Frequently Asked Questions

Is this medical advice?

No, this tool provides an educational estimate for a sample weight loss diet plan. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider or registered dietitian for personalized guidance.

How accurate are the calorie estimations?

The calorie estimations are based on general formulas that consider your input data. Individual metabolism, body composition, and lifestyle factors can vary significantly, so these are estimates and may not perfectly reflect your exact needs.

Can I customize the food items?

This tool generates a sample macronutrient and calorie target. The specific food items within those targets are up to you. Focus on whole, unprocessed foods that fit your dietary preferences and overall calorie goals.
