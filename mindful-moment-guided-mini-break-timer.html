Mindful Moment: Guided Mini-Break Timer

Refresh your focus and calm your mind with a short, guided pause.

Choose a duration between 1 and 10 minutes.

Your Guided Mini-Break:

How It Works

The Mindful Moment timer is designed to help you integrate short, refreshing breaks into your day. Simply choose how long you'd like your break to be, and we'll provide a simple, text-based guided exercise. These exercises are focused on gentle mindfulness or light stretching to help you reset your focus and reduce stress, all without needing external audio or video.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of exercises are included?

Our exercises are simple, text-based prompts focusing on either gentle mindfulness (like breath awareness or body scan) or light, seated stretches. They are designed to be easy to follow and require no special equipment or prior experience.

Can I use this tool on my phone?

Yes, the "Mindful Moment" tool is fully responsive and designed to work well on any device, including smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. Take your mini-break wherever you are!

Is this tool suitable for everyone?

While the exercises are generally gentle, if you have any health conditions or concerns, especially regarding physical movement, please consult with a healthcare professional before engaging in any new exercise routine. This tool is for educational purposes only.
