How It Works

Our Daily Micronutrient Gap Finder estimates potential nutrient deficiencies by analyzing your selected food groups against general dietary recommendations. It highlights key vitamins and minerals commonly found in those foods and suggests areas where your intake might be low. This is a simplified educational tool to raise awareness about diverse nutrition.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this tool?

This tool provides an educational estimate based on typical nutrient profiles of general food groups. It does not account for specific portion sizes, cooking methods, individual absorption rates, or unique dietary needs. For precise nutritional assessment, consult a registered dietitian or healthcare provider.

What if I eat foods not listed in the groups?

The food groups are broad categories designed to cover common dietary patterns. If you consume foods not explicitly listed, try to categorize them into the most appropriate group (e.g., lentils under "Legumes," salmon under "Seafood"). For highly specialized diets, this tool may be less representative.

Can this tool diagnose a deficiency?

No, this tool cannot diagnose any medical condition, including nutrient deficiencies. It is designed for educational purposes only to help you identify potential areas where your diet might be lacking. A diagnosis requires medical testing and evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional.

How often should I use this tool?

You can use this tool whenever you want to get a quick overview of your dietary patterns. It's helpful for reflecting on your food choices over a typical day or week. Remember, dietary needs can change, so regular reflection on your eating habits is beneficial.
