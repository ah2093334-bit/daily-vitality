How it Works

Simply enter your primary sleep concern, and our tool will analyze common recommendations. We provide personalized sleep position advice and pillow suggestions based on general wellness principles. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my concern isn't listed?
If your specific concern isn't listed, try entering a related term or a broader category. For example, if you have "hip pain," try "hip pain" or "joint pain." The advice provided is general and may still offer helpful insights.
Is this medical advice?
No, this tool is for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. The information provided is based on general wellness knowledge and common recommendations. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider for any health concerns or before making any decisions related to your health or treatment.
How are the comfort and pressure scores calculated?
The comfort and pressure scores are estimates based on common sleep science principles and the provided concern. They are intended to give a general idea of how a position might affect comfort and pressure points, not as precise medical measurements.
