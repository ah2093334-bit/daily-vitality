How It Works

Simply select your dietary preferences, any allergies, and your primary health goal. Our tool uses this information to generate a balanced, whole-food focused meal plan for the week. It also includes tips for mindful eating to enhance your experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of foods are included in the plan?

The plan emphasizes whole, unprocessed foods such as fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, whole grains, and healthy fats. It aims to be nutrient-dense and supportive of your chosen goals.

Can I customize the meals further?

This tool provides a structured plan. For detailed customization, you can swap meals with similar nutritional profiles or adjust portion sizes based on your individual needs and preferences. It's a starting point for your healthy eating journey.

What does "mindful eating" mean in this context?

Mindful eating involves paying attention to your food, your hunger and fullness cues, and your eating experience without judgment. Our plan incorporates gentle reminders and tips to help you cultivate a more conscious relationship with food.
