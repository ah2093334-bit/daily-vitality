Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended daily sodium intake?

For most healthy adults, the recommended daily sodium intake is less than 2,300 milligrams (mg). However, for individuals with high blood pressure, diabetes, or chronic kidney disease, and for most adults over 50, an ideal limit is often 1,500 mg per day. Always consult with a healthcare professional for personalized dietary advice.

How accurate are these sodium estimates?

The estimates provided by this tool are based on average sodium content for common food items. Actual sodium content can vary significantly depending on brands, preparation methods, and specific recipes. This tool is designed for educational purposes to give you a general idea, not precise nutritional tracking. For exact figures, always refer to food labels.

Why is managing sodium intake important?

Managing sodium intake is crucial for maintaining healthy blood pressure. High sodium consumption can lead to high blood pressure (hypertension), which increases the risk of heart disease, stroke, and kidney disease. Reducing sodium can help lower blood pressure and improve overall cardiovascular health.
