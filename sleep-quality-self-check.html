How It Works

This tool provides an educational estimate of your sleep quality based on your self-reported habits and feelings. By answering a few simple questions, you'll receive a score and personalized tips to help you understand and potentially improve your sleep. Remember, this is for informational purposes only and not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the sleep score mean?
The sleep score is an estimate from 0 to 100, indicating your perceived sleep quality based on your answers. Higher scores suggest better sleep quality. It's designed to give you a general idea and encourage reflection on your sleep habits.
Is this tool a substitute for medical advice?
No, this tool is for educational and informational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have concerns about your sleep or health, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
How are the personalized tips generated?
Tips are generated based on common sleep hygiene recommendations and your specific responses. For example, if you report difficulty falling asleep, you might receive tips on creating a relaxing bedtime routine.
