Screen Time Before Bed: A Practical Wind-Down Plan

Quick overview

A realistic way to reduce late-night screen stimulation without giving up your phone completely.

What this guide covers

  • The key idea in plain language
  • Practical habits
  • What common advice can and cannot tell you
  • When professional advice matters

Why late screens feel stimulating

Bright light, alerts and emotionally engaging content can keep the brain active.

The goal does not have to be zero screen time.

Create a realistic cutoff

Choose one point when work and high-stimulation content stop.

Consistency is more useful than an extreme rule.

Make the phone less exciting

Lower brightness and silence unnecessary notifications.

Small friction changes make it easier to disengage.

Pair screens with a cue

Dim the room, stretch, shower or read something calm.

A repeatable cue helps the evening transition feel natural.
