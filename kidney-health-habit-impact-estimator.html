Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy kidney? (Educational only, not medical advice)

Healthy kidneys are vital organs that filter waste products from your blood, regulate blood pressure, and produce hormones. Maintaining a healthy lifestyle supports their function.

How much water should I drink daily? (Educational only, not medical advice)

General recommendations suggest around 8 glasses (64 oz or 2 liters) of water per day, but individual needs can vary based on climate, activity level, and health conditions. Staying hydrated is crucial for kidney function.

What is considered a high sodium diet? (Educational only, not medical advice)

A diet high in sodium is generally considered to be one that exceeds the recommended daily limit, often cited as less than 2,300 mg per day. Excessive sodium can contribute to high blood pressure, which can affect kidney health.
