    Frequently Asked Questions

    What kind of activities are included?

    The planner includes a variety of gentle movement activities designed to promote well-being without being strenuous. Examples include walking, stretching, gentle yoga, Tai Chi, and mindful breathing exercises.

    Is this a substitute for medical advice?

    No, this planner is for educational and planning purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with your doctor or a qualified healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program.

    Can I customize the plan further?

    Yes, this planner provides a starting point. Feel free to adjust the activities, time slots, and frequency based on your personal preferences, energy levels, and what feels best for your body each day.
