Heart Rate Zone Calculator

Training zones are percentages of your maximum heart rate. They help you choose the right effort for fat burning, endurance or speed.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your age.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Match your workout effort to a zone; most cardio sits in the light to moderate zones.

Frequently asked questions

Which formula is used?

The Tanaka formula: 208 minus 0.7 times age.

Do medicines change heart rate?

Yes, for example beta blockers. Ask your doctor for your own limits.

Is 220 minus age better?

It is simpler but less accurate for many adults.
